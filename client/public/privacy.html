Privacy Policy

Algo Trade Simulator · Last updated 30 September 2026

Algo Trade Simulator is an educational paper-trading and backtesting app. This page explains what information it collects when you use it and what happens to that information.

What we collect

  • From your Google account, when you sign in: your name, email address and profile photo. We do not receive your password or access anything else in your Google account (no Gmail, contacts, Drive or other data).
  • Sign-in activity: the time of each sign-in and your browser and device type.
  • What you create in the app: simulations, strategy backtests and chat messages you send to the trading copilot.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your simulations and backtests attached to your account.
  • So the app's owner can see who has used the app and when (for example, number of visitors and recent sign-ins).
  • Chat questions are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate answers, together with public market data.

We do not sell your information, show you ads, or share it with anyone except the services below.

Where it is stored

The app runs on Vercel and stores data in a MongoDB Atlas database. Market prices come from Yahoo Finance; no personal information is sent to Yahoo.

Your choices

  • You can use the app only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • You can remove access at any time from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • To ask for your account and data to be deleted, or for any privacy question, open an issue at github.com/varun-sharma-2006/AlgoTrade/issues.

Not financial advice

Everything in the app, including backtests and chatbot answers, is for education only and is not financial advice.
